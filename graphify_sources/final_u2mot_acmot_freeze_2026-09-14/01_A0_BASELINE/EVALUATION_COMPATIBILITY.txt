Evaluation compatibility note

Environment:
NumPy 2.1.3
motmetrics 1.4.0

Problem:
motmetrics 1.4.0 calls np.asfarray.
np.asfarray was removed in NumPy 2.

Runtime-only compatibility alias:
np.asfarray(a, dtype)
=
np.asarray(a, dtype=dtype)

The official U2MOT eval_visdrone.py file was NOT modified.

Tracking predictions were NOT modified.

Ground-truth annotations were NOT modified.

Evaluation protocol was NOT modified.
